The Project

Giving a Local Favorite a Louder Voice

The Tutoring Center helps students build confidence and real academic skills, but their social channels were inactive. With An E stepped in to bring new content that reflected the center's wonderful work.

We partnered with The Tutoring Center to build a social media strategy and produce content to get the word out. Not only did we revitalize their Facebook, we also helped them create new Instagram and TikTok accounts so they could engage with a whole new audience.

The result: more eyes on their content, more time spent watching it, and more families picking up the phone.
