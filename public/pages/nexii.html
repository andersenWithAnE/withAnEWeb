The Project

Visualizing What Hasn't Been Built Yet

Nexii Construction is building the next generation of eco-friendly structures, but communicating complex architectural concepts to clients and stakeholders is a challenge all on its own.

We partnered with Nexii Construction to create a suite of architectural visualizations that bring their projects to life before any ground is broken. Our renderings not only show how Nexii Construction's buildings look in 3D space, but how they interact with the environment around them, day and night.

What We Delivered

From Blueprint to Big Picture

Architectural Visualizations

Detailed, photorealistic 3D renders of Nexii Construction's building projects, showing exteriors, structural systems, and material finishes in a way that flat drawings simply can't communicate.

Concept Videos

High-fidelity views of Nexii Construction's projects animated to show every layer being assembled, and showing a complete day-night cycle of the structure. We provided in-context visualizations showing not only Nexii Construction's buildings, but how they fit into the community.

Brand Support

Polished, on-brand slideshows, documents, and videos giving Nexii Construction a ready-to-go brand toolkit for client meetings, investor conversations, and project proposals.

Eye-Catching Video

Existing footage reimagined with new motion graphics.

With An E produced the footage using Nexii Construction b-roll, and we built the motion graphics and editorial treatment to showcase how Nexii Construction can deliver faster, smarter, more sustainable buildings.
